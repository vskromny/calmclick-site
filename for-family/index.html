Set it up for your parent

Worried that your mum or dad could fall for a scam text, email or letter? CalmClick gives them a calm second opinion. They take a photo or screenshot of the message, and the app explains in plain words what it seems to be and suggests a calm next step. Setting it up together takes a few minutes.

Download on theApp Store

Free to download. For iPhone. 5 free checks a month; optional paid Plus subscription.

Set it up together

  1. On your parent's iPhone, open this page and tap the App Store button to install CalmClick.
  2. Open the app together once, so they know where it is and what it looks like.
  3. Try it on a message you both know is harmless, so they see what a result looks like before a real one matters.
  4. Agree on one simple rule: if the result says “Caution” or “Warning”, or if money or a code is involved, they call you or someone they trust first.

What your parent sees

A plain explanation of what the message seems to be, without jargon, and a three-level read: “No clear warning signs”, “Caution”, or “Warning”, each with an icon and a word, not colour alone. “No clear warning signs” does not mean safe. When the app is unsure, it says so and tries to lean toward caution. Your parent can ask a follow-up question in their own words, and the result is written so it doesn't blame them.

What it doesn't do

CalmClick looks only at the photo your parent chooses. It does not read their messages in the background, it does not block calls or messages, and it does not replace you, their bank or the police. Think of it as a calm first look that helps them pause before they reply, click or pay.

Their photo and their checks

The photo they choose is sent through our relay to OpenAI in the US to be analyzed. OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days, then deletes it. Details are in our Privacy Policy. Any follow-up question is sent the same way. Free: 5 checks a month. Plus, an optional paid subscription, allows up to 60 checks a day. The price is shown in the App Store before anyone buys.

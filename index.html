How it works

Three steps. About ten seconds. No accounts, no jargon.

1

Take a photo

Point your phone at anything confusing — a message, a letter, an email, a pop-up — or upload a screenshot.

2

Get a calm explanation

CalmClick tells you what it is in plain words, in your language — never alarmist, never technical.

3

Know what to do

Clear "what to do" and "what not to do" steps. Still unsure? Ask a follow-up question, or reach someone you trust with one tap.

A simple traffic light, not a lecture

Every check ends in one of three honest verdicts. When CalmClick isn't sure, it says so — and errs on the side of caution.

⚠️ Be carefulThis could be a trick. Check before acting.

Privacy comes first

CalmClick is built for people who are careful about their phone — so it's careful too.

  • No monitoring, ever. CalmClick never reads your texts, calls, or email. It only sees a photo you choose to take.
  • Your results stay on your phone. Saved checks live only on your device. No account, no cloud copy, no tracking.
  • Photos are analyzed, then gone. A photo is sent securely to an AI service only to be read, is never used to train AI, and is deleted after a short safety period.

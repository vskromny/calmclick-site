Take a photo, get a plain answer

A message about a parcel, a bank, or a payment can be hard to judge. Take a photo or a screenshot with CalmClick and it reads the message for you. You get a short explanation in plain words, not jargon.

What you will see

CalmClick looks for common warning signs, such as pressure to act fast, odd links, or requests for money or codes. The result is a three-level read: “No clear warning signs”, “Caution”, or “Warning”, each with an icon and a word. When it is unsure, it says so and leans toward caution.

What to do next

You get simple steps: what not to tap, pay, or share, and how to check with the real company yourself, using a phone number or website you already trust. Still unsure? Ask a follow-up question or send the explanation to someone you trust.

Checking first is the right move

Scam messages are made to look convincing. Checking before you act is a sensible step. There is no account to create and there are no ads.

Your photo and your checks

The photo you choose is sent through our relay to OpenAI in the US to be analyzed. OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days, then deletes it. Details are in our Privacy Policy. Any follow-up question you ask is sent the same way. CalmClick never reads your messages in the background. Free: 5 checks a month. Plus, an optional paid subscription, allows up to 60 checks a day.

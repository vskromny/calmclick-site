Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 July 2026

CalmClick helps you understand confusing things on your screen — suspicious texts, odd bills, scary pop-ups — by explaining a photo you take in plain language. This policy explains, in the same plain language, what information the app handles and what happens to it.

The short version

  • CalmClick has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no tracking.
  • The app never monitors your phone. It never reads your messages, email, calls, contacts, or location. It only sees a photo you deliberately take or choose.
  • Your results and history are stored only on your device; we keep no copy of them on any server.
  • A photo you check is sent securely to an AI service (OpenAI) once, only to be analyzed, is not used to train AI models, and is deleted by that service after a short safety-review period (up to 30 days under OpenAI's API policy).

What information the app handles

1. Photos you choose to check

When you tap the button and take a photo (or pick one from your library), that photo is sent over an encrypted connection, through our relay server, to OpenAI's API, which reads the image and produces the explanation you see. This is the only moment any data leaves your phone.

  • Our relay server does not store your photo — it passes it along and returns the answer. The relay briefly uses your IP address in memory to limit abuse (rate limiting); it is not stored. Like any web service, the relay's host (Vercel) may process ordinary technical connection data, such as your IP address and the time of the request, to run and protect the service.
  • OpenAI processes the photo to generate the explanation. Under its API terms, content is not used to train AI models and is retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, then deleted. You can read more in OpenAI's own policies.
  • A copy of the photo may be saved on your device together with the result, so you can look back at it. You can delete it at any time.

2. Results and history

Explanations you choose to save ("checks") are stored only on your device. You can delete any check with a swipe, export your checks yourself, or turn on Auto-clear history to remove checks older than 30 days automatically. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

3. Follow-up questions

If you ask a follow-up question about a result, the question is sent to the same AI service to be answered, under the same rules as photos: encrypted, not used for training, deleted after the safety period.

4. Trusted contact and profile details

If you (or a family member) add a name, language preference, or a trusted person's phone or email in the app, those details are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere. Calling or texting your trusted person happens through your phone's normal Phone and Messages apps.

5. What we don't collect

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers.
  • No location, contacts, messages, call logs, or browsing history.
  • No account — we couldn't tie data to you even if we wanted to.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — only to take the photo you want checked. iOS asks for your consent first, and you can change it any time in Settings.
  • Photo library — only when you choose to upload an existing screenshot, using Apple's picker, which shares only the photo you select.

Children

CalmClick is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country — up to 16 in parts of the EU/EEA under Article 8 of the GDPR — please ask a parent or guardian before using the app.

Who is responsible for your data

CalmClick is made and provided by Vladimir Skromny, a private individual based in Switzerland, who is the data controller for the limited processing described in this policy. You can reach the controller at v.skromny@gmail.com.

Our legal basis

For users in the EU and EEA, we rely on the GDPR: we process the photo you submit to carry out the check you have asked for (Article 6(1)(b) — a service you request), and to keep the service secure and prevent misuse (Article 6(1)(f) — our legitimate interest in a safe service). For users in Switzerland, the same processing is governed by the revised Federal Act on Data Protection (revDSG/nDSG). iOS asks for your consent before the app uses the camera or your photo library.

Where your photo is processed

While a photo is being checked, it passes through our relay server, hosted by Vercel Inc., and on to OpenAI, L.L.C. Both are companies in the United States, so your photo may be processed there for the short time the check takes. These transfers rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses in the providers' data-processing terms, together with the Swiss addendum recognised by the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner. Neither company keeps your photo beyond the short periods described in section 1 above.

Your rights

You can ask to see any personal data relating to you, and to have it corrected, deleted, or its processing restricted; you can object to processing; and you can ask to receive your data in a portable form. Because your checks, history and profile are kept only on your device, you can already view, export and delete all of them yourself inside the app. For the brief processing that happens at OpenAI, email v.skromny@gmail.com and we will help.

If you believe your data has been handled improperly, you may lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC / EDÖB, www.edoeb.admin.ch) or, in the EU/EEA, with your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will be described in the app's update notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy — or anything else? Email v.skromny@gmail.com. We answer every message.

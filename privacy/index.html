Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

CalmClick helps you understand confusing things on your screen — suspicious texts, odd bills, scary pop-ups — by explaining a photo you take in plain language. This policy explains, in the same plain language, what information the app handles and what happens to it.

The short version

  • CalmClick has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no tracking.
  • The app never monitors your phone. It never reads your messages, email, calls, contacts, or location. It only sees a photo you deliberately take or choose.
  • Your results and history are stored only on your device; we keep no copy of them on any server.
  • A photo you check is sent securely to an AI service (OpenAI) once, only to be analyzed, is not used to train AI models, and may be kept by OpenAI for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, then deleted.
  • To keep the free checks fair, the app sends an ID that iOS gives our apps on your phone along with each check, so our relay can count how many checks come from one phone each day. It is not your name or account, it is not used for tracking or advertising, and the ID and the count are deleted within 2 days after your last check.

What information the app handles

1. Photos you choose to check

When you tap the button and take a photo (or pick one from your library), that photo is sent over an encrypted connection, through our relay server, to OpenAI's API, which reads the image and produces the explanation you see. Together with the photo, the app sends the device ID described in section 5. Nothing else leaves your phone.

  • Our relay server keeps no copy of your photo — it passes it along and returns the answer. The relay runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.). Like any network provider, Cloudflare processes technical connection data, such as your IP address and the time of the request, to deliver and protect the service. We have switched off Cloudflare's request logging for our relay.
  • OpenAI processes the photo to generate the explanation. Under its API terms, content is not used to train AI models and is retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, then deleted. You can read more in OpenAI's own policies.
  • A copy of the photo may be saved on your device together with the result, so you can look back at it. You can delete it at any time.

2. Results and history

Explanations you choose to save ("checks") are stored only on your device. You can delete any check with a swipe, export your checks yourself, or turn on Auto-clear history to remove checks older than 30 days automatically. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

3. Follow-up questions

If you ask a follow-up question about a result, the question is sent to the same AI service to be answered, under the same rules as photos: encrypted, not used for training, deleted after the safety period.

4. Trusted contact and profile details

If you (or a family member) add a name, language preference, or a trusted person's phone or email in the app, those details are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere. Calling or texting your trusted person happens through your phone's normal Phone and Messages apps.

5. A device ID from iOS, to keep free checks fair

With each check, the app sends our relay an ID that iOS gives to all apps from the same developer on your phone (Apple's "identifier for vendor"). The relay uses it for one thing only: to count how many checks come from one phone each day, so the free checks stay fair for everyone and the service is protected from misuse.

  • It is not your name, account, email, or an advertising ID. It is not used for tracking or advertising.
  • We do not store it with your photo. We have no name, email or account for you, so there is nothing to connect it to.
  • It is the same in all of our apps on your phone, so deleting CalmClick alone does not change it. iOS gives a new one when all of our apps have been removed from the phone and one is installed again; a new phone also gets a new one.
  • Of our apps, only CalmClick sends this ID to us, and we do not combine it with data from our other apps. If that ever changes, we will update this policy before it happens.
  • If the ID is not available, the relay counts checks by your IP address instead, in the same way.
  • The relay keeps only the ID (or IP address) and the number of checks for the current day (UTC). This counter is deleted automatically within 2 days (48 hours) after that phone's last check.

The app tells you about this ID in the short notice it shows before your first check.

6. What we don't collect

  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers. The only identifier the app sends is the device ID in section 5.
  • No location, contacts, messages, call logs, or browsing history.
  • No account — you never give us your name or email to use the app.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — only to take the photo you want checked. iOS asks for your consent first, and you can change it any time in Settings.
  • Photo library — only when you choose to upload an existing screenshot, using Apple's picker, which shares only the photo you select.

Children

CalmClick is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country — up to 16 in parts of the EU/EEA under Article 8 of the GDPR — please ask a parent or guardian before using the app.

Who is responsible for your data

CalmClick is made and provided by Vladimir Skromny, a private individual based in Switzerland, who is the data controller for the limited processing described in this policy. You can reach the controller at v.skromny@gmail.com.

Our legal basis

For users in the EU and EEA, we rely on the GDPR: we process the photo you submit to carry out the check you have asked for (Article 6(1)(b) — a service you request). We use the device ID, or your IP address if no ID is available, to count checks per phone per day, based on our legitimate interest in keeping the free checks fair and preventing misuse (Article 6(1)(f)). For users in Switzerland, the same processing is governed by the revised Federal Act on Data Protection (revDSG/nDSG); for the daily counter we rely on our overriding interest in fair use and abuse prevention (Article 31 revDSG/nDSG). iOS asks for your consent before the app uses the camera or your photo library.

Photos can show other people's details, such as a sender's name or phone number. We process them only to produce the explanation you asked for, based on our legitimate interest in providing that check (GDPR Article 6(1)(f); Article 31 revDSG/nDSG). Our relay keeps no copy. If you think your details appear in a photo and want to ask about it, email v.skromny@gmail.com.

CalmClick is not built for health information or identity documents. If a photo contains such information, it is processed in the same way and with the same limits.

Where your photo is processed

While a photo is being checked, it passes through our relay, run by Cloudflare, Inc., and on to OpenAI. Your photo is processed in the United States; OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days, unless a legal obligation requires longer, then deletes it; the daily counter described in section 5 is also held by Cloudflare. Our data processing agreement for OpenAI's services is with OpenAI Ireland Ltd. (OpenAI's contracting entity for customers in Switzerland and the EEA). It states that OpenAI transfers data outside the EEA and Switzerland under EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision, and OpenAI's US affiliate processes the photo there. Cloudflare, Inc. (United States) relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses in its data processing addendum, incorporated in its self-serve terms. Neither company keeps your photo beyond the periods described in section 1 above, except where the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask to see any personal data relating to you, and to have it corrected, deleted, or its processing restricted; you can object to processing; and you can ask to receive your data in a portable form. Because your checks, history and profile are kept only on your device, you can already view, export and delete all of them yourself inside the app. For the processing at OpenAI (photos kept there for up to 30 days, see section 1), email v.skromny@gmail.com and we will help.

Your right to object to the daily counter (Article 21 GDPR; revDSG/nDSG): you can object at any time. In practice, simply stop running checks, or email v.skromny@gmail.com. Either way, the counter for your phone is deleted within 2 days.

If you believe your data has been handled improperly, you may lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC / EDÖB, www.edoeb.admin.ch) or, in the EU/EEA, with your local data protection authority.
